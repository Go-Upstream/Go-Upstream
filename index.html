We help founders go upstream_

Go Upstream AB, founded by George Brontén, helps entrepreneurs on their journey — as a thinking partner on strategy, product, and go-to-market — and sometimes as an angel investor. Our home turf is technology, first and foremost SaaS.

About

Built by an operator, for operators

Go Upstream is not a fund with a clock ticking. It is the personal company of George Brontén — a serial entrepreneur who has spent 25+ years building software companies from first line of code to profitable scale and exit — created to share that journey with other founders.

What we bring is operating experience: product strategy, go-to-market for complex B2B sales, pricing, hiring, and the patience to build companies that last. When the fit is right, we also invest — but the partnership always comes first.

What we look for

Founders who solve problems at the root

We partner selectively, mostly in Nordic B2B SaaS, at stages where operating experience makes a real difference — and when the fit is right, we invest as angels. If this sounds like you, we'd like to hear from you.

  • B2B SaaS with recurring revenue Products that solve a painful, well-understood problem for business customers — ideally with early proof of willingness to pay.
  • Founder-led and mission-driven Entrepreneurs with deep domain insight, who care about craft and customers more than hype cycles.
  • Capital-efficient growth Companies built to become profitable, not just to raise the next round.
  • A long-term mindset We hold for years, not quarters — and we prefer partners who think the same way.
